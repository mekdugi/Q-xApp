Why a supplement run
--------------------
The main batch (seeds 1..30) produced 29/30; seed 16 was excluded
(BATCH_PARTIAL_1_30, failed_seeds=16) because both of its attempts failed
the v3.5 semantic validator on `qos_weight_applied=0`. A clean supplement
run of seed 16 (same code, same frozen binaries) was executed to fill the
missing seed. This is gap-filling of a failed seed with identical
code/binaries, not seed cherry-picking.

What the seed-16 failure was — and was NOT
------------------------------------------
Confirmable from preserved evidence:
  - The validator intermittently did not observe the scheduler's QoS-weight
    application marker for seed 16's failed attempts.
  - Re-running the SAME seed applies the weight normally (diag_s16/run_016
    via v2, and fig4_batch_v4_s16 attempt 2, both have the marker and PASS
    the v3.5 validator), so this is NOT a seed-/policy-deterministic failure.
  - A transient in the RC delivery or application path is the likely cause.
NOT confirmable (do not state as fact):
  - The exact failure point. The original FAILED-attempt logs were deleted
    by the batch on failure and were not preserved; the snapshot that exists
    (s16_attempt_snapshot/.tmp_run_016_a2) is the SUCCEEDING attempt and has
    the weight marker. Future investigation must preserve failed-attempt logs.
